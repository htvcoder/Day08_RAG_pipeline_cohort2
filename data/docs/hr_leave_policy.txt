# HR Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive annual leave according to their contract and local labor law. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR system at least 3 working days in advance when possible.

## Sick Leave
Employees who are unexpectedly sick should notify their manager and file the leave record as soon as possible after the absence begins.

## Approval
Managers approve standard leave based on team coverage. HR supports exception handling for extended leave and compliance cases.

## Scope Note
This policy is not part of the IT helpdesk or refund workflow and can be used as an example of a non-central routing case.
